Status:
- **AGREED** — the user said yes; use it, but it can still change freely.
- **LOCKED** — the user said “lock that in” or confirmed when asked to lock. Never silently change a locked decision; explain conflicts and ask before changing it unless safety requires action.
- **PLACEHOLDER** — filled in by the planner so drafting can proceed; not yet confirmed by the user.
